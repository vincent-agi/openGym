# 1. Getting started

## Open openGym

Someone (you, a friend, or your gym buddy) runs openGym on a server and gives you a web address, for
example `https://gym.example.com`. Open it in your phone or computer browser.

You can also try the **live demo** first. It runs entirely in your browser on example data, so nothing you do
there is saved on a server.

## Three ways to sign in

When you open openGym for the first time you see three choices.

| Button | What it does | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign in with passkey** | Signs you in to a profile you already made. | Coming back, or a second device. |
| **Create new profile** | Makes a new profile and a passkey for it. | The first time. |
| **Continue without account** | Guest mode. Everything stays in this browser only. | A quick test. |

### What is a passkey?

A passkey replaces your password. When you create a profile, your phone or computer makes a secret key and
keeps it safe. Next time you sign in, you just confirm with **Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your
device PIN**.

- There is **no password** to remember or leak.
- The secret part **never leaves your device**. The server only stores the public part.
- It can be saved in your password manager or phone keychain (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager…),
  which helps you keep it if you change phones.

> **Important:** a profile has exactly one passkey, and openGym has no email or "forgot password" option.
> If you lose the passkey, nobody can open that profile again. Keep your passkey backed up in your
> password manager, and use [Export backup](09-your-data.md#back-up-your-data) now and then.

### Create your profile

1. Tap **Create new profile**.
2. Type a name (up to 40 characters). It is only a label.
3. If the server is **invite-only**, also type the **invite code** the owner gave you.
4. Tap **Create passkey** and confirm with your face, finger or PIN.

You are in. If you were using guest mode before and already had workouts on this device, openGym moves that
data into your new profile.

### Sign in on a second device

On the new device, open the same address and tap **Sign in with passkey**. Your browser will offer the
passkeys saved in your password manager or keychain. Pick yours. Your plan, workouts and weight history
appear.

### Guest mode

Guest mode needs no account. It is fine for a trial, but:

- Data lives **only in this browser**. Clearing the browser data deletes it.
- There is no sync between devices.
- Export a backup from time to time (see [Your data](09-your-data.md)).

You can create a profile later from **Settings → Account**.

## Your first five minutes

### 1. Load a ready-made plan (optional)

On the **Home** screen, a new account shows a *Welcome* card. Tap **Load starter plan (PPL)** to get three
routines: **Push Day**, **Pull Day** and **Leg Day**, placed on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
You can also find this under **Settings → Data**.

Prefer to make your own? Tap **Build my own plan** and read [Plan and routines](02-plan-and-routines.md).

### 2. Log your body weight

On Home, tap **Log** in the *Body weight* card, set your weight with the slider or by typing, and save.
Tap **Goal** to set a target weight. The target is drawn as a line on your weight chart.

### 3. Start a workout

Tap **Start** in the bottom bar. openGym:

1. asks for a quick weigh-in (you can skip it with **Start without weighing in**);
2. opens today's workout with your weights and reps already filled in.

Tick each set when you finish it. The full walk-through is in [Working out](03-workouts.md).

## The Home screen

- **Week strip** — Monday to Sunday. A coloured dot shows the state of each day: trained, planned, or
  rescheduled. Tap a day to change what you do that day. Use the arrows to look at other weeks.
- **Today card** — shows today's routine. Tap it to **Start**, or **Resume** a running workout. On a rest
  day it offers to plan something.
- **Body weight card** — latest weight, change since the last weigh-in, your goal, and a 30-day chart.
- **Streak card** — your weekly streak and workouts this week. Tap it to open the calendar.
- **Nutrition card** — appears only after you have set calorie targets (see [Nutrition](06-nutrition.md)).

## Install it like an app

openGym is a *web app* that you can add to your home screen so it opens full-screen like a normal app.
See [Install on your phone](10-install-on-your-phone.md).

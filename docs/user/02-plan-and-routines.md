# 2. Plan and routines

Your plan has two parts:

- **Routines** — saved lists of exercises ("Push Day", "Legs", "Cardio").
- **Week schedule** — which routine you do on which weekday.

Open the **Plan** tab to see both.

## Week schedule

The **Week schedule** lists Monday to Sunday.

1. Tap a day.
2. Pick a routine, or pick **Rest day**.

That is your *weekly plan*. Gymme uses it to decide what **Start** does on each day and what shows on the
Home screen.

## Routines

### Create a routine

1. In **Plan**, under **Routines**, tap **New**.
2. Give it a name and tap the icon to choose a picture for it.
3. Tap **Add exercise** to fill it (see below).

### Add exercises

**Add exercise** opens the exercise picker. You can:

- search by name, muscle or equipment;
- filter by body part and by equipment (the equipment choices adapt to what you picked, so you never end up
  with an empty list);
- tap the **star chip** to see only the exercises you already chose;
- tap **Create your own exercise** if yours is not in the library (see [Exercise library](07-exercise-library.md)).

You can also add an exercise from the **Exercises** tab: tap **Plan** next to it, then pick a routine.

### Set up each exercise

Tap an exercise in a routine to change how it is done:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sets**, **Reps**, **Weight** | The plan for this exercise. |
| **Time** instead of **Reps** | For planks, hangs, wall sits and carries. You log seconds, not reps. You can still add weight. |
| **Bodyweight** | The exercise uses only your body. No weight column; you just log reps. Turn on a belt weight to log **Added** weight. |
| **Reps per side** | For lunges and one-arm rows. You log the total; Gymme shows "8 per side". The reps step by two so both sides stay even. |
| **Cardio** | For cardio exercises you set **Intervals**, **Minutes** and **Speed (km/h)**. |
| **Progression → Rule** | How the weight should change over time. See [Progression and 1RM](04-progression-and-1rm.md). |

### Supersets

A superset is two (or more) exercises done back-to-back, with rest only after the pair.

1. Put the two exercises next to each other in the routine.
2. Tap the **link button** on the second one ("Superset with exercise above").

During the workout they are shown together.

### Other routine options

- **Progression** (routine level) — a default rule for every exercise that does not set its own.
- **Playlist** — paste a Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music link. During the workout, a button opens it.
- **What this session hits** — a body diagram that shows which muscles this routine trains while you
  build it.
- **Delete routine** — removes the routine. Workouts you already logged keep their sets.

## Change a single day

Sick? Missed a session? Fewer gym days this week? You do not need to touch your weekly plan.

1. On **Home**, tap the day in the week strip.
2. Choose another routine, or **Rest / skip this day**.
3. To go back, choose **Back to weekly plan**.

That day shows as **rescheduled**. Only that date changes.

## The calendar

Tap the **streak card** on Home to open the calendar.

- **Trained** days are marked in your accent colour, **planned** days in grey and **rescheduled** days in orange.
- Tap a trained day to see its details (and delete the workout if you made a mistake).
- Tap any other day to plan a session for it.

## Share your plan

On the **Plan** screen, tap the **upload icon** in the top right.

| Button | Result |
|---|---|
| **Export plan file** | Saves a small file with your routines and week schedule. Send it to a friend. It contains **no workouts and no weigh-ins**. |
| **Print / Save as PDF** | A clean printout, one plan per page. No exercise is cut in half across two pages. |
| **Import a plan file** | Adds a friend's routines to your plan. |

Importing is safe: routines are **added as new ones**, and nothing you already have is changed. If you also tick
**Use this weekly schedule**, your Monday–Sunday assignments are replaced with theirs.

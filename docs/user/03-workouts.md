# 3. Working out

## Start a workout

Tap **Start** in the bottom bar (or the **Today** card on Home).

- If today has a routine, openGym starts it. You may see a **Start workout** screen first, showing *Today's plan*
  and **Other routines** you can pick instead.
- For a free session, choose **Freestyle workout (pick as you go)** and add exercises as you go.
- If a workout is already running, the button says **Resume** and takes you back to it.

### The quick check-in

Before the workout starts, openGym asks for your body weight so your weight curve stays honest.
Set it with the slider or type it, then tap **Save & start workout**.

Not today? Tap **Start without weighing in**, or **Choose a different workout**.

## The workout screen

You see one exercise at a time, with an animation that shows how to do it.

- Top: **Exercise 3 / 6**. Use **Prev** and **Next** to move between exercises.
- Tap the **info icon** next to the name for written instructions and your best weight.
- **Last time** shows what you did before.
- **Discard** (top left) throws the workout away. You are asked to confirm.
- If the routine has a playlist, the **playlist button** opens it.
- A **screen that stays awake** is on by default, so your phone does not lock between sets.

### Log a set

Each row is a set. The numbers are **already filled in** from your plan and from last time, adjusted by your
progression rule (see [Progression and 1RM](04-progression-and-1rm.md)). A short note under the exercise
tells you *why* the number is what it is.

1. Change the weight or reps with **−** and **+**, or tap the number and type.
2. When you finish the set, **tick it**.
3. A **rest timer** starts.

Other buttons:

- **Add set** and **Remove set** change the number of sets for today only.
- **Add exercise** adds something extra to this session.

### The rest timer

After you tick a set, a bar shows the countdown.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **−15s** | Shorter rest. |
| **+30s** | Longer rest. |
| **Skip** | End the rest now. |

The rest length is set in [Settings](08-settings.md) (60 to 180 seconds). If push notifications are on, you also get an
alert when the rest is over — even if the app is closed or your screen is off.

If your routine has a playlist, openGym can offer to resume the music when rest ends (switch off in Settings).

### After the last set of an exercise

openGym asks **"Confirm the weight you worked with"**. Your highest weight becomes the starting point next time.
It tells you if you beat your **previous best** ("new record!"). Skipped for bodyweight exercises, which have
no weight to confirm.

## Different kinds of exercises

### Timed exercises (planks, hangs, wall sits)

Instead of reps, you log **seconds**. Tap **Start set**: a work timer counts the set. Tap **Done** when you stop
and it logs the time you actually held. This timer is separate from the rest timer.

### Bodyweight exercises

Push-ups, pull-ups and dips have **no weight column**. You only log reps. If you add a belt or vest in the
exercise settings, a column named **Added** appears.

### Reps per side

For lunges and similar exercises, you log the **total** reps. The screen shows the split, for example
"8 per side".

### Left and right sides

Some exercises let you log each side on its own row. Tap the number at the start of the row to switch between
**Left**, **Right** and **Both**. Left and right personal records are tracked separately, so a strong side
cannot hide a weak one.

### Supersets

Exercises in a superset are shown together: *Superset 1 / 2*. Do one set of each, then rest only after the pair.

### Cardio

You log **Duration (min)** and **Speed (km/h)** instead of weight and reps.

## How hard was the set? (RIR and RPE)

You can add an optional third column that records **how hard each set felt**. It is **off by default**.
Turn it on in **Settings → During a workout → Effort per set**.

| RIR (reps in reserve) | RPE (scale of 10) | How it felt |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 10 | Nothing left — you went to failure |
| 1 | 9 | One more rep possible |
| 2 | 8 | Two more reps |
| 3 | 7 | Three more reps |
| 4 or more | 6 or less | Easy, like a warm-up |

Pick the scale you already think in. They are the same idea counted from opposite ends: **RPE ≈ 10 − RIR**.
Most working sets land around RIR 2 / RPE 8.

Effort is for your information. It does **not** change your progression or your 1RM estimate.

## Finish a workout

When you tick the last set, openGym offers **Finish workout** or **Continue workout** (to add more).
You can also tap **Finish** at any time. If sets are still unticked, openGym asks you to confirm.

The **Workout complete!** screen shows:

- duration, volume (weight × reps), number of sets and number of PRs;
- **New PR** lines for exercises where you lifted more than ever before;
- **Best estimated 1RM** lines when you did the same weight for more reps;
- **How hard did that feel?** — an optional 1–10 rating for the whole session;
- **What you just trained** — your muscle map for this session.

## Mistakes and changes

- **Wrong weight in a finished workout?** Open it from the calendar or **Stats → Recent workouts** and check it.
  You can delete a workout from its detail sheet.
- **Closed the app in the middle?** The running workout is kept **on that device**. Open openGym and tap **Resume**.
  A workout in progress is not synced until you finish it.

## For people who train with limited mobility

Settings has an **Accessibility & mobility** section that hides exercises that do not suit you, makes buttons
bigger during workouts, and can remind wheelchair users to do pressure relief. See [Settings](08-settings.md).

# 5. Progress and stats

Everything about your progress lives in the **Stats** tab, plus the body-weight card on **Home**.

## Body weight

- Log your weight any time with **Log** (Home or Stats), or at the quick check-in before a workout.
- Tap **Goal** to set a **target weight**. It appears as a line on the chart.
- Changes are coloured by whether they move you **toward** your goal or away from it, so a drop is good when
  you are cutting and a rise is good when you are gaining.
- The chart on Stats can show **1M**, **3M**, **1Y** or **All**.

> The kg / lb setting only changes the **label**. If you switch units, your old numbers are **not converted**.
> Pick your unit before you start logging.

## Stats at a glance

The top of **Stats** shows four tiles: total **Workouts**, workouts **This month**, your **Week streak**
and your **Weight 30d** change.

A **week streak** counts how many weeks in a row you trained.

## Activity heatmap

A year of training days, like the grid on GitHub. A darker square means more time spent training that day.
Tap a square to open that day's workout.

## Muscle map

A front-and-back body diagram that is shaded by how much work each muscle got. Choose a period (**Week**, **30d**,
**90d** or **All**) and how to measure it:

- **by sets worked**,
- **by hard sets** (sets close to failure — needs the effort column turned on),
- **by time trained**.

Gymme also lists the muscles you **have not trained** in that period, so you can fix gaps. Tap a muscle to
see more. While you build a routine, it previews which muscles that routine hits. After a workout, it shows what
you just trained. Pick a male or female figure in [Settings](08-settings.md).

## Effort chart

If you rate your sets with RIR or RPE, a card shows **where your sets land** — how close to failure your training
usually is — and an **average effort per workout** line. You need a few rated sets before averages appear
(fewer than five would be noise). See [Working out](03-workouts.md#how-hard-was-the-set-rir-and-rpe).

## Exercise progress

Pick any exercise you have done and see a curve. You can switch the line between:

| Option | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Top set** | The heaviest set weight per workout (for cardio: top speed; for holds: longest hold). |
| **Est. 1RM** | Your estimated one-rep max per workout. See [Progression and 1RM](04-progression-and-1rm.md#estimated-1rm). |
| **Effort** | Average effort per workout, if you rate your sets. |

Use **1M / 3M / 1Y / All** for the time range. Under the chart you see your recent sessions set by set.

## Goals

Besides the target weight, you can set **dated goals**. In **Stats → Goals**, tap **Add goal**.

| Goal type | Example | Progress is measured by |
|---|---|---|
| **Lift** | Squat 140 kg | Your best estimated 1RM on that exercise. |
| **Body weight** | Reach 75 kg | Your latest weight, from where you started. Works up or down. |
| **Volume** (workouts) | 12 workouts | Number of workouts since you created the goal (up to the deadline, if you set one). |

A **deadline** is optional. Each goal shows a progress bar.

## Measurements

In **Stats → Measurements**, tap **Log** to record any of these (all optional, so you can log only your waist):

- **Waist**, **Chest**, **Arms**, **Hips** (in cm)
- **Body fat** (in %)

You get one entry per day. Logging again on the same day adds to that day's entry without erasing the rest.
Each measurement has its own chart.

## History

Tap the **clock icon** at the top right of Stats to see **every workout** you have logged. Tap one to see its sets.
You can delete a workout from there if you logged it by mistake.

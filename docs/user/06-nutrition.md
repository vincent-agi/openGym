# 6. Nutrition

The **Nutrition** tab helps you track calories and macros (protein, carbs, fat). It is simple on purpose:
you type in what you eat. There is no food database or barcode scanner.

> Gymme gives **rough estimates for general fitness**. It is not medical or dietitian advice. If you have a
> health condition, ask a professional.

## Set your targets (first time)

1. Open **Nutrition**.
2. Choose your goal: **Cut** (lose fat), **Maintain** or **Bulk** (gain).
3. Choose your **Activity level**: Sedentary, Lightly active, Moderately active, Active or Very active.
4. Tap **Set targets**.

Gymme works out a daily **calorie** and **macro** target from your **latest logged body weight**. So log your
weight first. Without one, no targets can be made.

How it estimates (so nothing is a surprise):

- Calories = body weight × a number that depends on your activity level, then **−17.5 %** for a cut or
  **+12.5 %** for a bulk.
- Protein = **1.8 g per kg** of body weight.
- Fat = **25 %** of calories.
- Carbs = whatever calories remain.

It does not know your height, age or sex, so treat the result as a **starting point**.

> **Good to know:** the app shows the target form only until targets are set. There is no button yet to
> recalculate them afterwards. Someone comfortable with files can change them by editing a backup file — see
> [Editing a backup by hand](../technical/data-model.md#editing-a-profile-by-hand) in the technical docs.
> Do **not** use *Reset everything* for this: it deletes your workouts too.

## Log what you eat

1. Use the arrows at the top of the card to pick a day.
2. Under **Food log**, tap **Add food**.
3. Fill in **Name**, **Calories (kcal)**, **Protein (g)**, **Carbs (g)** and **Fat (g)**, then **Save**.

Tap a food to edit it, or the **bin** to delete it. The bars at the top show how close you are to each target.

## Plan meals ahead

Under **Planned meals**, tap **Plan a meal** to add a meal for a day. Planned meals **do not count** towards your
totals yet. When you eat one, tap the **tick** button (**Mark as eaten**): it is copied into your food log and
removed from the plan.

## On the Home screen

After you set targets, a card on Home shows today's calories and macros. Tap it to open Nutrition.

# 7. Exercise library

The **Exercises** tab holds **1,324 exercises**, each with an animation and written steps.

## Find an exercise

- **Search** — type a name, a muscle, a piece of equipment or a word from the description.
- **Body part chips** — tap *Arms*, *Back*, *Chest*… to narrow the list. **All** clears the filter.
- **Equipment chips** — appear under the body parts. They adapt to your search so every choice has results.
  Use **Any equipment** to clear them. This is how you show only what you own.
- Tap **Show more** at the bottom to load more results.

If you have logged an exercise before, your **best weight** is shown as a small tag on its row.

## Exercise details

Tap an exercise to see:

- the **animation**;
- **Best** and **last** weight you used;
- the **1RM calculator** (see [Progression and 1RM](04-progression-and-1rm.md#estimated-1rm));
- **How to** — step-by-step instructions.

Instructions are translated into English, Español, Français, Italiano, Türkçe, Русский, 中文, हिन्दी, Polski
and 한국어. For **Deutsch** and **Português** the screen is translated but the instructions stay in English.

Tap **Add to my plan** (or **Plan** on the row) and pick a routine.

## Create your own exercise

Cannot find it? Tap **Create your own exercise**.

1. Type a **name**.
2. Pick a **body part**. Choose *cardio* if it should log time and speed.
3. Optionally add a **description** (setup, cues, anything to remember).
4. Tap **Create exercise**.

Your exercise works like a built-in one in every screen. It has no animation. You can **edit** or **delete** it
later from its detail sheet. Deleting removes it from your routines, but workouts you already logged keep their sets.

## Exercises that fit your body

If you set an accessibility or mobility profile in [Settings](08-settings.md), the library only shows exercises
that fit it (for example, seated exercises). Leave the profile unset to see everything.

# 8. Settings

Open Settings with the **gear icon** at the top right of **Home**. Changes are saved at once and, if you are
signed in, synced to your profile (so they follow you to your other devices).

## Account

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your name | Shows the profile you are signed in as. |
| **Admin dashboard** | Only for the person who runs the server. |
| **Sign out** | Syncs your data, then clears it from this device. Your passkey keeps working. |
| **Sign out everywhere** | Ends your sessions on **all** your devices, this one included. Use it if a phone is lost or stolen. |
| **Create passkey profile** / **Sign in with passkey** | Shown in guest mode, to move to a real profile. |

In guest mode you see the note *"Guest mode — data lives only in this browser."*

## General

- **Language** — 12 languages.
- **Weight unit** — **kg** or **lb**. This only changes the label; numbers are not converted.

## During a workout

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rest timer** | 60, 90, 120, 150 or 180 seconds between sets. |
| **Keep screen awake** | Stops your phone from locking mid-workout. Needs a secure (HTTPS) connection, and iPhones refuse it in Low Power Mode. |
| **Sounds** | Beeps when you tick a set and when the rest ends. |
| **Playlist cue on rest end** | Offers to resume your routine's playlist when the rest is over. |
| **Effort per set** | **Off**, **RIR** or **RPE**. Tap the **(i)** for a plain explanation. See [Working out](03-workouts.md#how-hard-was-the-set-rir-and-rpe). |

## Notifications

Needs a signed-in profile and a secure connection (not available in guest mode or on the mobile app's browser
version without HTTPS).

- **Push notifications** — rest-timer alerts, even when Gymme is closed. The browser asks for permission.
- **Workout day reminder** — a nudge at a time you choose, **only on days with a planned routine where you have not
  logged a workout yet**. It uses your own time zone and follows you when you travel.
- **Send test notification** — checks it works.

In the standalone Android/iOS app, the reminder is a local notification on the phone and needs no server.

## Accessibility and mobility

Optional. Leave it all unset to see every exercise.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mobility level** | Full, Partial, Wheelchair user, or Limited mobility. |
| **Preferred posture** | Seated (wheelchair), Seated (chair), Lying down / bed, or Standing. |
| **Limbs not trained** | Left or right leg, left or right arm — exercises that need them are hidden. |
| **Large touch targets** | Bigger buttons during a workout and on the rest timer. Turned on automatically once you set any mobility option; you can switch it off. |
| **Pressure-relief reminders** | Only for wheelchair profiles. A gentle nudge during rest to shift your weight or do a chair push-up, every 10, 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes of session time. A button shows the exercise. |

## Appearance

- **Theme** — Dark or Light.
- **Body diagram** — Male or Female figure on the muscle map.
- **Accent color** — eight colours: lime, sky, orange, violet, pink, red, teal and gold.

## Data

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Load starter plan (PPL)** | Adds Push / Pull / Legs routines on Mon / Wed / Fri. |
| **Import from another app** | FitNotes, Strong, Hevy — or body weight from Apple Health. |
| **Import backup** | Restores a JSON backup. **Replaces** all current data. |
| **Export backup (JSON)** | Downloads everything as one file. |
| **Reset everything** | Deletes your plan, workouts and body weight on this device. Cannot be undone. |

Details are in [Your data](09-your-data.md).

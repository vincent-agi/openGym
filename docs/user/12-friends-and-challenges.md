# 12. Friends and challenges

Gymme can help you train with friends. It is built to **motivate, not to compete**: you compare yourself with your
own plan, cheer each other on, and nobody ever sees your weight, your food or your workouts.

> The friends features are **off until you turn them on**. If you never open the section, nothing about you is
> shared and nothing changes in the app. The person who runs your server can also switch the whole module off.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Friends & sharing**.
2. Pick a **Handle** (3 to 20 letters a–z, digits or `_`) and a **Display name**. Friends find you with the handle.
3. Tap **Share with friends**.

The card **What friends will see** shows exactly what a friend can see, and it updates as you change the switches.
You can turn sharing off at any time: you disappear from your friends' screens at once.

### What friends can see

Only a short summary that the server computes from your training. You choose which parts to share:

| Switch | What it shares |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | How many sessions you did this week and this month, and which days you trained (no times, routines or exercises). |
| **Consistency** | Sessions done compared with **your own plan**, and a comparison with your own recent weeks. |
| **Week streak** | Consecutive weeks with at least one session. |
| **Personal records** | How many records you broke recently. Off by default. |

**Never shared, whatever you choose:** body weight, measurements, food, calories, how hard a set felt, your mobility
profile, exercise names, weights and reps, or why you took a break.

## Add friends

Open **Crew** (from the **Crew** card on Home, or **Settings → Friends & sharing → Open Crew**).

- **Share your code:** tap **Add**, then **Create my friend code**, and send the link. A code works for 14 days and
  for as many friends as you like. Creating a new code cancels the old one.
- **Use a friend's code:** tap **Add**, type or paste their code, link or `@handle`, and tap **Send request**.
  If you open their link while signed out, sign in first and the request is sent for you.
- **Requests:** a request appears under **Requests**. Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.

If you ask someone who has already asked you, you simply become friends.

For privacy, Gymme gives the **same answer** whether a handle does not exist, the person is not sharing, or they blocked
you. If a request "does not work", that is normal and nothing is revealed.

**Remove or block.** **Manage** removes a friend. **Block** removes them and stops them from finding you or asking
again, without telling them. **Blocked** lists the people you blocked, where you can **Unblock** them.

## The Crew leaderboard

**This crew** is a friendly list for the **Week** or the **Month**. Sort by:

- **Consistency** (default, weekly only): sessions done compared with *your own* plan, so someone planning three
  sessions and doing three is level with someone planning five and doing five.
- **Sessions**: how many sessions.
- **Streak**: weeks in a row.

Ties share a position, and the only thing that breaks a tie is the name. Someone with no session yet, nothing planned
or no recent activity is shown with a neutral sentence, never as "last". If you turn on **Hide my rank**, you see
the list without positions, and your friends see you without a position too.

Under a name you may also see a small **"+1.5 sessions a week vs usual"**: a comparison with that person's own past,
never with others. Only encouraging figures are shown to friends; a dip is shown only to you.

## Take a break without losing your standing

Ill, travelling or injured? In **Settings → Friends & sharing → Plan a break**, choose the dates (up to 14 days).
Those days are taken out of your plan, so your consistency is neither lowered nor credited for them. Nothing is shared
about why. Sessions you still do during a break count.

## Challenges

A challenge is a goal over **7 to 90 days** for 2 to 12 people. In **Crew → Challenges**, tap **New** and start from a
ready-made template:

| Type | It counts |
|---|---|
| **Stick to the plan** (default) | The weeks in which you did the sessions you planned. Fair whatever your level. |
| **Sessions** | Sessions done. |
| **Active days** | Days you trained. |
| **Weeks in a row** | The longest run of weeks with a session. |

Choose **Friendly duel** (everyone chases the same target and the list is ranked, ties allowed) or **Together**
(everyone's progress adds up to one shared target, with no ranking). You can invite your accepted friends who are
sharing. You can be in up to **5** challenges at once.

- Invited people see an **Invitation**; tap it to **Join** or **Decline**. You can **Leave** at any time, and
  rejoin while it is still running.
- If you join late, you count from the day you joined.
- If you turn sharing off, you are shown as **Paused** and the days spent paused never count once you are back.
- Everything is counted from your saved sessions on the server, so nobody can add points by hand.
- When a challenge ends, its results are frozen.

## Cheers and activity

**Activity** shows when friends finished a session, with no detail about what they did. Tap the cheer button to
send one of five emoji (👏 🔥 💪 🎉 ❤️). You can send one per session, change it, or take it back. There is no
free text anywhere. On your own last session you see the cheers you received. In **Crew → Friends**, **Mute cheers** hides one friend's cheers from you, without telling them.

## Notifications

In **Settings → Friend notifications** (after turning on notifications in the section above), choose what may reach you:
a friend trained, someone cheered you, challenge invitations, milestones and results. **All are off by default.**
Nothing is sent during your quiet hours (21:00 to 08:00 unless you change them), at most one "a friend trained"
notification arrives a day (friends who train together are grouped), and no more than three social notifications a day.
Messages are always encouraging: no rankings, no "you are behind".

> Notifications are written in your app's language when it is French or English; other languages receive English for now.

## Badges and your weekly recap

**Your badges** (in Crew) are small rewards for habits and kindness, never for strength or for winning:
**First week**, **Hat-trick** (three sessions in a week), **Four in a row**, **Back on track** (you came back after a
break or a missed week), **Team player** (a co-op challenge reached its target) and **Cheerleader** (ten cheers sent).
Friends see **none** of your badges until you switch one on.

On Monday to Wednesday, Home can show **Your week**: last week's sessions against your plan, your streak, a challenge and what
your crew did so far. Tap **Dismiss** for this week or **Don't show again**.

## Leave and erase

**Settings → Friends & sharing → Leave and erase friends data** removes your friends, requests, challenges, cheers
and badges from the server and turns sharing off. Your own workouts are **not** touched. You can set everything up
again later. Your backup file (see [Your data](09-your-data.md)) keeps your own friends *settings*, but importing it
never restores friends or challenges.

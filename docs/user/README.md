# openGym user guide

openGym is a workout and body-weight tracker. You plan your week, follow a guided workout on your phone,
and watch your progress grow. Your data stays on a server you or a friend control — there is no company
in the middle, no ads and no subscription.

This guide is for **people who train**. If you run the server, see the
[technical documentation](../technical/README.md) instead.

## The big picture in one minute

1. **Sign in** with a *passkey* (Face ID, fingerprint or your device PIN — no password) or try openGym as a **guest**.
2. **Plan** your week: make *routines* (for example "Push Day") and put them on weekdays.
3. Tap **Start** on a training day. openGym asks for your body weight, then opens the workout with your weights already filled in.
4. **Tick each set** when you finish it. A rest timer starts by itself.
5. Check **Stats** to see your streak, charts, personal records and which muscles you have been training.

## Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Routine** | A saved list of exercises, such as "Pull Day". |
| **Set** | One group of reps, for example 10 push-ups. |
| **Rep** | One repetition of an exercise. |
| **Superset** | Two exercises done back-to-back, with rest only after the pair. |
| **PR** | Personal record — your best weight so far on an exercise. |
| **1RM** | The heaviest weight you could lift once. openGym can estimate it. |
| **RIR / RPE** | Two ways to say how hard a set felt. See [Working out](03-workouts.md#how-hard-was-the-set-rir-and-rpe). |
| **Passkey** | A login stored on your device and unlocked with your face, finger or PIN. |
| **Guest mode** | Using openGym without an account. Data stays in that one browser. |

## Screens at a glance

The bar at the bottom of the screen has six buttons:

| Button | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your week, today's workout, body weight, streak. The gear icon (top right) opens Settings. |
| **Plan** | Your week schedule and your routines. |
| **Start** (centre) | Starts today's workout. Shows **Resume** if a workout is already running. |
| **Stats** | Charts, heatmap, muscle map, goals and measurements. |
| **Nutrition** | Calories, macros and meal plans. |
| **Exercises** | The exercise library. |

openGym can be used in 12 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Polski,
Türkçe, Русский, 中文, 한국어 and हिन्दी. Change it in [Settings](08-settings.md).
